One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch entry for the make.fun bounty of the same name. `main.ts` writes exactly one sentence to `/output/entry.txt` — a pitch for a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance. Fully offline and deterministic: no network, no clock, no randomness, standard library only.

> My earphones untangle themselves overnight by holding tiny couples therapy sessions for their knots, so each morning they wake up reconciled, single, and ready to listen.

Why this pitch: tangled earphones are the classic tiny annoyance, and the fix is silly yet weirdly plausible — the knots hold tiny couples therapy sessions overnight, so by morning they have reconciled and come apart on their own.

node main.ts
